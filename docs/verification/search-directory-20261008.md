# Independent tenant and contract search — 8 October 2026

R01.05 continuation: the published contact search repair only covered the selected rent-office month. This candidate adds a read-only directory from the signed-in user's existing `aqari_tenants`, `aqari_leases`, `aqari_units` and `aqari_properties` access. It finds tenants without a current rent row and historical contracts without requiring a matching collection period. No schema, roles, keys or business records change.

`src/v267/api/record-search.js` checks the server's workspace/user/role and section permissions, pages ordered records with an explicit workspace filter, respects existing RLS, rejects unexpected scopes, and joins only accessible tenant/property/unit/lease identities. Phone (at least eight digits) and exact twelve-digit civil-ID matching normalize Arabic/Persian digits transiently. Results retain no raw identifiers, profiles, balance or payment state. The 10,000-row read cap raises an incomplete-search error rather than silently truncating.

`v209-global-search.js` combines this directory with existing unit and monthly results. Reader failures preserve successful results with an incomplete-results notice. Monthly financial actions remain on their original verified rows. Independent records open the tenant timeline only, after the exact tenant and any lease/unit/property relationship are reread under current access. Opening the selected tenant reuses `src/v267/pages/tenant-timeline.js`; account loss disposes its private DOM. Search changes, month changes, panel close and auth seal cancel both readers and discard late results.

Validation: `tests/v267-record-search.test.mjs` exercises localized identifiers, historical and unlinked tenants, section denial, RLS-hidden relationships, pagination, unexpected workspaces, account changes/cancellation, revalidation of moved/revoked identities and opening the actual tenant timeline. `tests/v209-behavior.test.cjs` covers cross-month matches, independent reader failure, cancellation, escaped labels, denied financial actions and deduplication with monthly results. The existing search, unit and owner-reference regressions also pass (59 focused tests). The prepared release regression suite passed 2,733 tests with zero failures/skips; package and diff checks passed.
