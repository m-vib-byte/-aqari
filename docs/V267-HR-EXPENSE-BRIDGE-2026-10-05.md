# Salary disbursements in the expense register

R18.05 continuation after PR409. New paid salaries with an issued allocation snapshot receive one immutable private link keyed by payroll ID. It records the original signed HR document, net paid amount, reference, voucher, payee and Kuwait payment date. Property lines use the immutable allocation captured at issue. No duplicate manual expense or property invoice is created.

The financial register shows these read-only salary lines with a separate net-disbursement total and distinct payroll count. Existing manual expense summary fields keep their meaning. Closing a future financial period captures salary disbursements and unlinked legacy salary count alongside the existing manual/rent snapshot. This is a cash-disbursement view, not gross salary accrual, cash balance or profit accounting.

Both payment and expense approval reject a previously used noncash payment reference within the workspace; HR transfer maps to financial bank. The existing workspace serialization lock prevents an approval/payment race through the RPCs. Cancelling a manual expense preserves its original record and releases its reference. Cash transactions cannot be deduplicated by an absent reference. A shared bank batch reference across separate salaries needs separate reviewed support; it must not be entered as separate payments with an identical reference.

Existing salaries are not backfilled or assigned invented historic shares. They appear as an unlinked count requiring historical reconciliation. Existing closed periods and business records are untouched. The salary correction flow remains document-only: it does not reverse a real payment. Financial reversal/adjustment and reconciliation of older salaries remain open under R18.05; this release does not claim the entire requirement complete.

Validation: isolated PostgreSQL and hosted Preview rollback fixture use actual salary issue, two approvals, signed-document finalization and paid RPC. They cover reference collisions in both directions, cancelled expense release, replay, source linkage, immutable/private link, property isolation, two properties sharing one payment with exact fils, Kuwait midnight boundary, financial close snapshot, and legacy upgrade without backfill. Storage fixtures are metadata-only, not actual file-upload or physical-device acceptance. The frontend regression checks read-only salary actions, separate totals, filtering and denial redaction. All 2410 JavaScript tests pass locally. Hosted synthetic workspace/users/links are zero after rollback.
